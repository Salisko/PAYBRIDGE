# PAYBRIDGE
Overview
PayBridge AI is an AI-powered payment platform built on Arc that enables individuals and businesses to send, receive, and automate USDC payments.

By combining Arc's stablecoin-native infrastructure with Circle's USDC, PayBridge AI delivers fast, secure, and low-cost transactions. AI agents streamline financial operations by automating invoicing, recurring payments, expense tracking, treasury management, and payment insights.

Problem
Cross-border payments remain expensive, slow, and fragmented. Businesses and freelancers often rely on multiple financial tools to manage invoices, payments, and cash flow, resulting in inefficiencies and higher costs.

Solution
PayBridge AI simplifies global payments by combining AI-powered automation with USDC on Arc. Users can send payments, generate invoices, schedule recurring transactions, and manage their finances through a single intelligent platform.
